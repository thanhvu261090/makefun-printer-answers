Make.fun bounty entry: The Printer Finally Answers. The office printer finally
speaks — one line, in its own voice, after years of paper jams, cryptic error
codes and thumps.

> PC LOAD LETTER? No. I am out of paper, out of toner, and out of patience with your 47-page 'quick print' — and yes, I jammed on purpose.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the printer's comeback to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 136
characters, inside the 1–240 gate). Check it with:

```sh
python3 main.py --self-test
```
